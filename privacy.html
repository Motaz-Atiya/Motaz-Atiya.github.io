Privacy policy

Life OS Tasks is a private app used only by its owner.

It accesses the owner's Google Tasks with his permission and uses that data only for him. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
